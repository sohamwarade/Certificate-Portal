What makes a good template
  • No sample name, dotted line, or "Name:" text where the name will go.
  • A clear gap as wide as your longest name.
  • At least 2000 px wide. A4 landscape at 300 dpi is 3508 × 2480.
  • One flat PNG or JPG, not a PDF.
